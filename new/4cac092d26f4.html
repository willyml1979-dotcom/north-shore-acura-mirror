Sporty Sophistication with Premium Versatility! The 2025 Acura Integra Elite A-Spec CVT delivers a perfect blend of dynamic design, advanced technology, and everyday comfort. Powered by a spirited 1.5L turbocharged VTEC engine producing 200 hp, this sleek sportback offers responsive performance with a smooth Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT) and paddle shifters for added driver engagement. The A-Spec styling package adds bold flair with gloss black accents, a sporty rear spoiler, 18-inch alloy wheels, and exclusive badging for a head-turning look. Inside, the Elite trim elevates the experience with leather and Ultrasuede-trimmed seats, a 9-inch touchscreen with built-in navigation, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and a premium ELS Studio 3D audio system. With the AcuraWatch safety suite, adaptive cruise control, and head-up display, the 2025 Integra Elite A-Spec CVT is designed for drivers who want refined performance, cutting-edge tech, and standout style—all in one exhilarating package.
