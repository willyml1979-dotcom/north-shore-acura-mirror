Experience top-tier luxury, advanced technology, and confident all-weather performance with the 2026 Acura MDX Platinum Elite A-Spec SH-AWD, a premium three-row SUV engineered for Canadian drivers who demand refinement with a sporty edge. Powered by a 3.5-litre i-VTEC V6 engine producing 290 horsepower and 267 lb-ft of torque, paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission and Acura’s Super Handling All-Wheel Drive system, the MDX delivers strong acceleration, precise handling, and excellent traction in all road conditions. Inside, the Platinum Elite A-Spec trim surrounds you with a high-end, driver-focused cabin featuring perforated leather and Ultrasuede sport seating, a flat-bottom heated steering wheel, ambient lighting, a panoramic sunroof, and a premium 19-speaker Bang & Olufsen audio system, along with a 12.3-inch digital cockpit and touchscreen infotainment with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto for seamless connectivity. With spacious seating for up to seven, versatile cargo space, and a full suite of AcuraWatch™ safety technologies including adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping assist, blind spot monitoring, and collision mitigation braking, the MDX delivers both luxury and peace of mind on every drive. Combining bold A-Spec styling, cutting-edge tech, and everyday practicality, the 2026 MDX Platinum Elite A-Spec SH-AWD stands as one of the most refined and capable SUVs in its class.
