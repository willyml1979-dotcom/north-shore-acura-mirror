This all-new 2026 Acura MDX A-Spec SH-AWD is a sporty, high-tech luxury SUV that blends aggressive design, next-level comfort, and drive confidence for today’s discerning driver. Powered by a refined 3.5L V6 engine coupled with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission and Acura’s dynamic Super Handling All-Wheel Drive™, it delivers a spirited performance with controlled handling—perfect for spirited everyday driving or challenging weather. The A-Spec package elevates its presence with bold Shark-Gray 20-inch alloy wheels, darkened exterior accents, LED fog lights, and a flat-bottom sport steering wheel with paddle shifters. Inside, the cabin boasts premium Milano leather and Ultrasuede® upholstery with contrast stitching and metal sport pedals. Every drive is immersive thanks to Google-built-in connectivity, a panoramic moonroof, and a 19-speaker Bang & Olufsen premium audio system that brings crisp, powerful sound throughout the three-row layout. With seating for seven, a versatile & spacious second row, and AcuraWatch safety tech under contract, the 2026 MDX A-Spec SH-AWD delivers athletic elegance, plush comfort, and modern tech in one refined package.
