Electrified Performance, Sport-Scarred Style! The 2025 Acura ADX A-Spec unleashes a youthful, athletic spirit in a compact luxury crossover, blending racetrack-inspired cues with everyday versatility. Under the hood, a potent 1.5L VTEC® turbocharged engine teams with a smooth CVT and dual-mode Sport Drive System to deliver 200 hp and razor-sharp throttle response. A-Spec styling turns heads with gloss black brake ducts, a Diamond Pentagon grille, and 19-inch Shark Gray wheels wrapped in performance tires. Inside, the cockpit is driver-centric and richly appointed—Ultrasuede™ trimmed sport seats cradle you through corners, while red contrast stitching and aluminum pedals underscore its performance pedigree. Tech is front and center with a 10.2-inch HD display, wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, and built-in Alexa for seamless connectivity. AcuraWatch™ safety features like Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keeping Assist, and Blind Spot Warning give you confidence on every road. With its aggressive stance, premium touches, and engaging drive, the 2025 ADX A-Spec is engineered to thrill both daily commutes and weekend escapes.
