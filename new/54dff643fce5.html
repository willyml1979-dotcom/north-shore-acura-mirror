Experience sporty design, refined comfort, and modern technology with the 2026 Acura Integra Elite A-Spec CVT, a premium compact sport sedan crafted for Canadian drivers who want everyday practicality with a dynamic edge. Powered by a responsive 1.5-litre turbocharged VTEC four-cylinder engine producing 200 horsepower and 192 lb-ft of torque, paired with a smooth continuously variable transmission with paddle shifters and front-wheel drive, the Integra delivers lively acceleration, excellent fuel efficiency, and a composed, engaging drive for both city and highway travel. Inside, the Elite A-Spec trim elevates the experience with sport-inspired styling, premium leather and Ultrasuede seating, a fully digital instrument cluster, a large touchscreen infotainment system with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and a premium audio system that creates a refined, driver-focused cabin. With its versatile liftback design, spacious interior, and AcuraWatch™ safety suite including adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping assist, and collision mitigation braking, the Integra offers both everyday usability and peace of mind. Combining sharp styling, advanced tech, and a balanced blend of performance and efficiency, the 2026 Integra Elite A-Spec CVT stands out as a modern sport compact that delivers premium value and driving enjoyment.
